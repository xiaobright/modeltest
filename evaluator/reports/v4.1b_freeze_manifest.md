## 5. Interpretation Policy

- Ability >=95 with no serious Ship blocker is the reliable-completion band for
  the owner's current daily projects.
- Ability >=90 is a usable execution band; Ship and failed families still
  determine review needs.
- These thresholds are project-local empirical guidance, not universal model
  certification.
- The scoreboard's worst-of-n main column is retained for historical continuity.
  Mixed n is not statistically fair: n=1 rows remain provisional, and worst is
  only the lowest observed delivery.
- DeepSeek gray and preview are separate routes and must never be merged.
- Costs retain their original actual/equivalent/cache caveats and do not affect
  Ability.

## 6. Post-Freeze Policy

1. Do not change V4.1b scoring, fixtures, Gold semantics, or formal scores.
2. Do not recompute V4.0 or V4.1a using V4.1b.
3. New model runs may be appended only as new observations under the frozen
   rules; they do not reopen the 2026-07-18/19 anchor set.
4. Do not create V4.1c to distinguish 95-99 with marker/reason/report details.
5. V5 remains a long-term memo and starts only when real development presents
   important failure modes that V4.1b does not predict.
